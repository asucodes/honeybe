Page summary

honeybee ranks 220 public, paid, open HackerOne programs by payout reliability, crowding, triage quality and scope breadth. Data comes from free, unauthenticated sources and refreshes every six hours.

Programs with insufficient signal are marked unknown instead of scored favorably. Missing data never becomes a good score.
