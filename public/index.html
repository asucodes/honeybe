Prize size is not the signal.

The biggest number on a program page is not the number most hunters can win. honeybee ranks on the three things that decide whether a weekend turns into a payout.

01 · Will it pay?

Payout behaviour, not promises

Response efficiency, bounties paid in the last 90 days, self-missed SLAs and dormancy. Programs that miss their own targets are marked.

02 · Can you get in?

Crowding, per asset

Raw report counts punish huge scopes. honeybee divides reports by assets, so a broad program is not treated like a crowded one.

Built on measured behaviour.

The weights are not a vibe. Each primary axis comes from published research on how bounty hunters and programs actually behave.

Age

Maillart et al.

Discovery probability decays as t^-0.40 after launch, measured across 35 public HackerOne programs.

arXiv:1608.03445
Hunter priorities

Akgul et al.

In a survey of 159 hunters, responsiveness and payout disputes beat prize size as reasons to leave a program.

arXiv:2301.04781
Fairness

Zheng et al.

Across 9,942 reports, reputation won 59.85% of near-identical pairs. honeybee does not model it, because you cannot see it fairly.

arXiv:2511.18608
